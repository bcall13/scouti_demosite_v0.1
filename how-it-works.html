Step 01 — Capture & Upload

Bring Your Own Video

No heavy upfront hardware investments. No forced ecosystem lock-ins. Whether your club records with a Veo, a Trace, a GoPro, or a sideline camera — simply upload your 90-minute match file to our secure cloud and SCOUTi takes it from there.

Any camera, any format. We accept broadcast, drone, wide-angle, and smartphone footage without conversion or reformatting.
Secure cloud upload. Your footage lives in an encrypted environment — private to your club, accessible to your staff.
Zero new hardware required. The camera you already own is all you need. No vendor contracts, no equipment leases.

Step 02 — Automated AI Processing

The Assistant Analyst

While you sleep, our spatio-temporal AI engine goes to work. We track the continuous flow of the game — not just the moments the ball moves, but the 89 minutes around them. Defensive shape. Off-ball support. Spatial occupation. Pressing triggers. We don't just count passes — we quantify the intangibles.

Every player, every minute. Full 90-minute tracking per player — movement, positioning, and decision-making mapped continuously.
Spatio-temporal intelligence. Our engine measures relative positions, distances, and angles — the geometry that determines outcomes before the ball arrives.
No human in the loop required. Fully automated, overnight. Your results are ready Saturday morning.
Step 03 — Actionable Delivery

Your Tuesday Practice Plan

Wake up to a highly digestible tactical dashboard. Instead of a spreadsheet of raw data, SCOUTi delivers an objective summary of your team's performance — complete with prescriptive, actionable next steps ready to drive your next training session.

Player-level development profiles. Individual metrics for every player — what improved, what needs work, and exactly how to address it.
Prescriptive next steps, not raw data. Specific drills and coaching cues tied directly to what the footage showed.
Recruiting profiles built automatically. The same verified metrics your coaches use become each player's discoverable profile.
Output: Tactical Dashboard · Player Development Cards · Team Heatmaps · Prescriptive Drill Recommendations · Verified Player Profiles
