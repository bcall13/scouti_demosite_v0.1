// Measure the Unmeasured

Quantify the Intangibles.

Move beyond basic box scores. SCOUTi tracks the 89 minutes a player doesn't have the ball — the behaviors that win or lose matches before the first touch.

89min

The Minutes That Matter Most

A player has the ball for an average of 1–2 minutes per match. Everything else — the positioning, the pressing, the rotations, the spacing — happens in the other 89 minutes. Traditional platforms measure the 1 minute. SCOUTi is built around the 89.

// Core Capabilities

Four Features.
One Intelligence Layer.

Each SCOUTi capability turns a specific gap in traditional analysis into a measurable, actionable output.

Feature 01

Defensive Integrity Tracking

Automatically flag missed defensive rotations and measure backline spacing to ensure your team moves as a cohesive unit — not a collection of individuals reacting to the ball.

Backline compactness measured continuously throughout transitions and set phases
Rotation adherence scored per player — who held shape and who broke it
Timestamped breakdown events linked directly to match footage for instant review
Feature 02

Off-Ball Support Metrics

Measure the distance and angles of teammates during possession phases. Identify exactly who is creating passing lanes and who is leaving the ball carrier isolated.

Support angle quality scored per possession sequence across the full match
Pass lane availability tracked — how many viable options each ball carrier had
Player-level support scores normalized by position and tactical role
Feature 03 — Core Metric

Pitch Control & Work Rate

Translate raw tracking data into standardized 1–100 player card metrics. Objectively grade a player's spatial footprint and effort — normalized by their specific position so comparisons across roles are always fair.

Pitch control score captures territorial dominance and positional occupation over time
Work rate index measures distance covered, sprint frequency, and pressing intensity
Position-normalized benchmarking — your CM is always compared to other CMs
Feature 04

Prescriptive Next Steps

Don't just stare at the stats — fix them. SCOUTi's intelligence layer translates game data into recommended drills and actionable coaching feedback designed to immediately improve player development.

Drill recommendations tied directly to flagged metrics — not generic suggestions
Player-level action items generated per match — specific enough to run on Tuesday
Week-over-week tracking confirms whether prescribed interventions are working
